# Concept Visualizer — Animation Script (visual-only)

Companion to `docs/CLASS-SCRIPT-2HR.md`. Open `docs/concept-visualizer.html`, full-screen it.
**The screen never carries your explanation as text** — only icons, shapes and motion. You
speak the explanation live (condensed from the full class script below each scene).
Advance with **click / Space / →**, back with **←**, **Esc** for the scene grid.

**Tomorrow's split:** Scenes 1–5 (Agent loop, Good prompt, Context/hallucination, Harness,
Fix-it formula) are for the **live hour** — you drive these in front of the class. Scenes
6–10 (MCP, Agentic AI/rules/secrets, Verification, Git/CI-CD, Recap) are the **self-paced
companion** — hand students the link and let them click through these themselves while they
finish Blocks 5–8 at home.

## Scene 1 — What is an AI Agent? (Block 1)
**On screen, in order:**
1. A boxed 💬 icon appears, sealed — it cannot move.
2. Beside it, a 🤖 icon appears; small hand icons reach out and pull in a 📄 and a ⌨️.
3. 🧠 badge appears at the top of a circle.
4. The circle draws a quarter-arc clockwise; ⚡ badge lands at the right.
5. Another quarter-arc draws; 👁 badge lands at the bottom.
6. The final arc closes the circle; 🔁 badge lands at the left — a glowing dot starts
   **continuously travelling around the circle**, forever.

**Say (brief, off-screen):** "ChatGPT only talks — sealed box. An agent has hands: it grabs
files and the terminal. Then it just loops: think, act, observe, repeat — forever, until the
job's done. That circling dot never stops — that's literally what's running right now while
your own agent builds."

---

## Scene 2 — Anatomy of a Good Prompt (Block 2)
**On screen, in order:**
1. A blank card outline appears, empty.
2. A 🎯 stamp thuds onto the top-left corner.
3. A red colour swatch block drops into the top-right corner (the exact hex, shown as a
   colour, not a word).
4. A 🚧 fence stamp slams onto the bottom-left.
5. A ✅ stamp lands on the bottom-right.

**Say (brief):** "A real prompt is a brief with four stamps: a goal, an exact spec — not
'red', this exact red — a fence of constraints, and a way to check it's done. Skip a stamp,
and the AI fills the blank itself."

---

## Scene 3 — Context & Hallucination (Block 3)
**On screen, in order:**
1. An empty glowing box appears, labelled nothing.
2. A 👤 icon flies in and lands inside with a bounce.
3. A 💻 icon flies in and lands.
4. A 🎓 icon flies in and lands.
5. Outside the box, a flickering 👻 icon glitches, shakes, and snaps into a solid red
   building icon — unstable, jittering continuously.

**Say (brief):** "Everything inside that box is what the AI actually knows. The flickering
one outside — that's what happens when a fact is missing. It doesn't say 'I don't know.' It
invents something and holds it like it's true."

---

## Scene 4 — The Harness (Block 4)
**On screen, in order:**
1. An empty car outline fades in.
2. A glowing ⚙️ badge drops into the engine position.
3. Two 🛞 badges slide in from the left.
4. A 🎛️ badge drops into the dashboard position.
5. A 🛑 badge slides in from the right.
6. Two miniature car silhouettes in different colours fade in side-by-side below, same
   engine glow, different shapes.

**Say (brief):** "Same glowing engine every time — that's the model. Wheels, dashboard,
brakes — tools, context, permissions — that's the harness. Different shape, same engine:
that's why Antigravity and Cursor feel different."

---

## Scene 5 — The Fix-It Formula (Checkpoint)
**On screen, in order (an actual mock phone screen, animated):**
1. A simple phone outline appears with two shapes overlapping inside it (a circle "portrait"
   sitting on top of a pill "button").
2. A 🔍 magnifying glass scales in over the overlap.
3. A ⚠️ badge flashes on the overlap zone.
4. The circle **physically slides down** below the pill and shrinks to ~70% size — the
   overlap resolves live, on screen.
5. A dashed fence outline draws itself around the rest of the phone screen, locking it.

**Say (brief):** "Watch — that's the whole fix, happening in front of you. Point at the
problem, flag it, move it, then fence off everything else so the agent doesn't touch what
already works."

## Scene 6 — MCP: USB-C for AI (Block 5)
**On screen, in order:**
1. A centre hub icon (🔌) appears, pulsing gently.
2. A line draws out to a 🐙 (GitHub) node; a small dot starts travelling back and forth
   along that line.
3. A line draws to a 🎨 (Figma) node; its own travelling dot starts.
4. A line draws to a 🗄 (Database) node; travelling dot starts.
5. A line draws to a ☁️ (Drive) node; travelling dot starts.

**Say (brief):** "One plug, four different tools, current literally flowing to all of them
at once. That's MCP — build the plug once, anything that speaks MCP can use it."

## Scene 7 — Agentic AI, Rules & Secrets (Block 6)
**On screen, in order:**
1. A 🧑‍💼 icon cross-fades into a 🤖 icon, with a small directional arrow between them.
2. A 📘 icon appears and visibly "opens" (two halves rotate outward).
3. A 🔑 icon appears; a 🔒 padlock snaps shut over it with a shake.
4. A 🚫 icon flashes briefly over a tiny GitHub mark.

**Say (brief):** "You direct, it builds. Rules are the handbook it reads every time. A key
is a password — lock it up, never let it reach GitHub."

---

## Scene 8 — Verification (Block 7)
**On screen, in order:**
1. A 🤖 speech-bubble with ✅ appears — then a big red ❌ stamps over it, shaking.
2. A 👆 icon bounces in; a green check badge pops onto it.
3. A 📱 icon bounces in; check badge pops.
4. A 🔍🐛 icon bounces in; check badge pops.
5. A 🔨 icon bounces in; check badge pops.

**Say (brief):** "It will say 'done' even when it's wrong. Every one of these checks is on
you now: click it, test it on your phone, hunt the errors, run the build."

---

## Scene 9 — Git & CI/CD (Block 8)
**On screen, in order:**
1. 💻 node appears.
2. Line draws to a 🔀 node.
3. Line draws to a 🐙 node.
4. Line draws to a ▲ node, which starts glowing; a dot begins travelling continuously
   left-to-right through the whole chain, and a small looping arrow pulses from the ▲ back
   to 💻.

**Say (brief):** "Watch that dot keep moving — every push replays this whole chain by itself.
That loop-back arrow is Vercel rebuilding itself the moment you push again."

## Scene 10 — Wrap-up Recap
**On screen:** six icon badges in a grid (🔁 🎯 🧠 🚗 🔌 ✅), dim at first. Each click lights
one badge gold, full brightness, pulsing once.

**You ask out loud, badge by badge** (same six questions as the full class script):
1. 🔁 — What is an agent?
2. 🎯 — What makes a good prompt?
3. 🧠 — What's context / hallucination?
4. 🚗 — What's a harness?
5. 🔌 — What's MCP?
6. ✅ — Who checks the AI's work?

**Say (brief):** "Six icons. Six ideas. You didn't just build a website — you now know how
every AI tool out there actually works."
